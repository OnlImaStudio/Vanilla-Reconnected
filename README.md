# Vanilla Reconnected

**Minecraft, as it should be.**

Vanilla Reconnected is a large-scale Minecraft mod currently in development.

It is focused on systematically refining and reconnecting the vanilla experience: resolving awkwardness, bringing conflicting rules into alignment, completing unfinished ideas, and improving the relationships between parts of the game that feel separated.

The goal is to make Minecraft feel more consistent, intuitive, and complete while preserving its identity.

## Status

In Development

Vanilla Reconnected has a broad and detailed design direction and is being implemented step by step.

## Project principles

- Keep rules coherent
- Make important rules readable
- Connect what already exists
- Preserve Minecraft's identity
